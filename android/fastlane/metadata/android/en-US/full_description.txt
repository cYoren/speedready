Speedready is a reader for people learning a language. It carries you through a book at a
pace you set, so you keep reading instead of stopping at every unfamiliar word, and it puts
the translation right where your eyes already are.

Above every word you have not marked as known yet sits its translation in your own language.
Tap that translation and the word is marked as learned: it disappears from the whole book,
and the text gets cleaner as your vocabulary grows.

<b>While you read</b>

* A highlight sweeps through the text at your pace, or flashes one word at a time
* Tap a word for its translation, to hear it, or to start reading from there
* Hold a word you are still learning: it is underlined everywhere and the pace slows for it
* Have sentences read aloud by the voice already on your device, with the text following along
* Bookmark a spot, jump between chapters, and pick up where you left off in every book

<b>It stays on your device</b>

The dictionary and the book ship inside the app. There is no account, no network access, and
the app requests no permissions at all. Everything you mark, learn and bookmark stays on your
phone, and everything you look up can be exported as an Anki deck.

<b>Included</b>

German to English and German to Portuguese, with a public-domain German storybook to start
reading immediately. You can open any EPUB or text file of your own.

Speedready is free software under the MIT licence. The same reader runs as a desktop
application on Linux.
