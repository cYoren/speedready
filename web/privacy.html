Privacy

Speedready for iPhone, iPad, Android, the web and Linux. Last updated 30 September 2026.

In short

Speedready collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you read, look up or mark ever leaves your device.

What stays on your device

  • The books you open. They are read on your device and never uploaded.
  • Where you are in each book, your bookmarks, and the words you mark as known or as still learning.
  • Your settings, such as speed, text size and theme.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. When you export your vocabulary for Anki, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Connections

The apps work without a connection: the dictionary and the starter book are built in. On the web, your browser downloads the reader once from this website, which does not set cookies or record who you are. Read-aloud uses the voice already installed on your device. Only if you tap a dictionary or source link does your browser open that outside website, under its own privacy policy.

Children

Speedready collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
